Scenario
A patient and a robot collaborate to produce a painting together, with the shared goal of
creating an artwork that reflects the patient’s mood. The robot contributes either by
suggesting or applying specific colors/shapes, while the patient directs the creative process.
Neither can achieve the final product alone, the robot provides structure/variation, the patient
provides meaning/emotion.
Research
● Research question : Does a proactive robot collaborator (suggests colors/shapes
unprompted) improve patient engagement compared to a reactive collaborator (only
acts after the patient directs it)?
● Hypothesis : Proactive behavior increases perceived collaboration quality and
reduces moments of stagnation.
● Method : Use Wizard-of-Oz, maybe in an online canvas where the participant can
draw and collaborate with the “robot”, the other user that is drawing. Run participants
through two painting sessions (proactive vs reactive). Collect self-reports
(engagement, enjoyment, trust) and maybe task-level metrics (number of
contributions, smoothness of turn-taking, quality of the artwork?). So a within-subject
design where the order of the conditions is randomized.
● Measures:
○ Subjective: Self-reports (engagement, enjoyment, trust, happiness with
result) on a likert scale (based on an existing method), analysis by using
one-way ANOVA to see whether there’s a significant difference between the
conditions
○ Objective: Time spent drawing total/pen on paper, idle time, analysis by
using one-way ANOVA to see whether there’s a significant difference
between the conditions
Task Description
The participant is asked to draw something that reflects their current emotions.
Robot states
● Proactive: e.g. start painting initially, potentially recognizable shapes to associate
with
● Reactive: user/patient starts painting, robot reacts and continues what they start
